About MedicoTech

Built to support healthcare professionals

MedicoTech is a healthcare productivity company that develops specialist-designed digital solutions and provides professional services when organisations require additional specialist capacity.

We believe healthcare professionals should spend less time carrying administrative burdens and more time focused on the work they are trained to do. By combining healthcare expertise, structured systems, and technology-enabled workflows, MedicoTech helps improve workflow efficiency, increase specialist capacity, and support better healthcare outcomes while maintaining full professional oversight.

What we do

Technology and specialist capacity, working together

MedicoTech develops digital solutions that support healthcare professionals in capturing, organising, analysing, documenting, and communicating information more efficiently.

Our solutions are designed to support healthcare specialists, healthcare organisations, and specialist-dependent environments where accuracy, efficiency, accountability, and professional judgement matter.

In addition to our technology platform, MedicoTech provides access to multidisciplinary healthcare specialists who can support organisations with consulting, patient engagement, assessments, reporting, and tailored institutional services when additional expertise is required.

Why we exist

More capacity for the work that matters

Healthcare professionals face increasing pressure from growing workloads, complex documentation requirements, limited capacity, and operational bottlenecks. These challenges can contribute to delays, backlogs, and reduced time available for direct patient care.

MedicoTech was established to help address this reality.

Our mission is simple:

Reduce administrative burden
Improve workflow efficiency
Increase specialist capacity
Support better healthcare outcomes

We believe technology should support professionals, not replace them. By helping healthcare specialists work more efficiently and consistently, we aim to create more time, focus, and capacity for the people who depend on them every day.

Our philosophy

Technology should support professionals.

We believe healthcare professionals should be empowered to focus on the work they are trained to do while technology supports the capture, organisation, documentation, and management of information behind the scenes.

Everything we build is guided by a simple principle: make work easier, protect professional integrity, and help healthcare professionals deliver their best work consistently and confidently.

That is why MedicoTech exists — to help healthcare professionals regain time, focus, and capacity while maintaining full professional oversight and accountability.

The founder

Thato Masia

MedicoTech was founded by Thato Masia, whose experience in clinical psychology and entrepreneurship highlighted a recurring challenge across healthcare environments: highly skilled professionals were spending significant time managing documentation, administrative tasks, and complex workflows alongside their clinical responsibilities.

What began as an effort to improve workflow efficiency within psychological practice evolved into a broader vision: creating digital solutions that support healthcare professionals across multiple disciplines while preserving the quality, integrity, and accountability of professional practice.

Today, MedicoTech continues to build technology in collaboration with experienced healthcare professionals, with the goal of helping individuals and organisations work more efficiently, increase capacity, and deliver better outcomes without losing sight of the people at the centre of healthcare.
