Privacy Policy

How MedicoTech collects, uses, and protects your personal information under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA), the National Health Act 61 of 2003, and applicable HPCSA guidelines for the handling of health information.

This Privacy Policy applies to the MedicoTech website and services. It is designed to comply with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA) of South Africa, the National Health Act 61 of 2003, and applicable Health Professions Council of South Africa (HPCSA) guidelines for the handling of health information. By using our services, you agree to the terms of this policy.

1. Introduction

TPT Medico Tech (Pty) Ltd, trading as MedicoTech ("MedicoTech", "we", "us", or "our"), is a healthcare consulting and reporting company based in South Africa. This policy governs personal information we collect through our website and general business interactions. Personal and health information processed as part of a specific consulting or reporting engagement is governed by the relevant services agreement and data-processing terms. We are committed to implementing reasonable measures to support compliance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), the National Health Act, and applicable HPCSA guidelines for the handling of health information. We apply data protection principles by design and by default across all systems, processes, and services we operate.

For the purposes of this policy, MedicoTech acts as the "responsible party" under POPIA in respect of the personal information described below. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and what rights you have in relation to your personal information.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information only when necessary for legitimate business purposes. The categories of personal information we may collect include:

Information You Provide Directly

  • Account registration details including name, email address, and contact number
  • Information submitted via contact forms, booking forms, and enquiry pages
  • Event registrations and webinar sign-ups
  • Support requests and service correspondence
  • Any other information you voluntarily provide when engaging with our services

Information Collected Automatically

  • IP address and approximate geographic location
  • Browser type, version, and device information
  • Pages visited, time spent, and navigation paths on our website
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies
  • Usage and performance analytics

Information from Third Parties

  • Data from technology partners and integrations
  • Referral information from partners and resellers
  • Lead and contact data from marketing platforms
  • Publicly available professional information such as LinkedIn profiles

Sensitive / Special-Category Information

We do not generally seek to collect special personal information (under POPIA), such as data revealing health, race, or religion, through our website. Where we do process such information, including health information handled as part of a consulting or reporting engagement, we do so only with your explicit consent or where otherwise permitted by law. We handle health information in line with the National Health Act and applicable HPCSA confidentiality guidelines, and limit its use and disclosure accordingly.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the personal information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide, operate, and improve our services and platforms
  • To communicate with you about your account, enquiries, and support requests
  • To send marketing communications where you have provided consent
  • To conduct analytics and improve the performance of our websites
  • To ensure the security of our platforms and prevent fraud
  • To comply with legal obligations and regulatory requirements

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We only process personal information where we have a lawful basis to do so under POPIA:

  • Consent, where you have given clear agreement for a specific purpose
  • Contractual necessity, where processing is required to deliver a service you have requested
  • Legal obligation, where processing is required by applicable law
  • Legitimate interest, where processing is necessary for our reasonable business purposes and does not override your rights

5. Your Rights

Under POPIA, you have the following rights in relation to your personal information. To exercise any right, please contact us using the details in Section 13. We will respond free of charge and through your preferred contact method where reasonable.

  • The right to access the personal information we hold about you
  • The right to request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • The right to request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal obligations
  • The right to object to the processing of your personal information, including via email, SMS, WhatsApp, telephone, or in person. All objections are free of charge
  • The right to withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent
  • The right to lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa. The 2025 amendments also allow third parties and public interest representatives to lodge complaints on your behalf
  • The right to opt out of direct marketing communications at any time through any reasonable channel

6. Direct Marketing

We send electronic marketing communications, including email, SMS, and WhatsApp messages, only where you have provided explicit, freely given, and recorded consent. In line with the POPIA Amendment Regulations of April 2025, our marketing consent process requires a positive opt-in action. We do not accept pre-ticked boxes, bundled consent, or opt-out mechanisms as valid consent for direct marketing.

Every marketing communication includes a clear and accessible mechanism to withdraw consent and opt out. Consent for electronic marketing is time-limited and reviewed periodically. You may withdraw consent at any time by contacting us or using the unsubscribe link included in all marketing messages.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as is necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by law, including any record-retention periods that apply to health records under the National Health Act and applicable regulations. When personal information is no longer required, it is securely deleted or anonymised.

8. Security and Data Sharing

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. Health information is handled in line with the National Health Act and applicable HPCSA confidentiality guidelines.

In the event of a security compromise where personal information may have been accessed or acquired by an unauthorised party, we will notify the relevant regulator and affected data subjects as soon as reasonably possible. Notifications will describe the nature of the compromise, the possible consequences, the measures we have taken or intend to take in response, and recommended steps data subjects can take to protect themselves. We maintain internal records of all security incidents and our responses to them, in line with the 2025 POPIA Amendment Regulations.

We do not sell personal information to third parties. Personal information may be shared with:

  • Trusted service providers who process data on our behalf under strict confidentiality agreements
  • Technology platform partners required to deliver our services, such as our reporting platform and cloud hosting providers
  • Regulatory authorities or law enforcement where required by law

9. Cookies

Our websites use cookies and similar technologies to improve user experience, analyse traffic, and support marketing. You can manage your cookie preferences through the consent banner displayed when you first visit our website. Essential cookies cannot be disabled as they are required for the website to function.

10. Children

Our services are not directed at individuals under the age of 18 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have collected information from a child without appropriate consent, we will delete it promptly.

11. International Transfers

MedicoTech is based in South Africa and processes personal information in South Africa. Where personal information is transferred to or processed by service providers outside South Africa, we ensure the recipient is bound by terms providing a comparable level of protection, as required under section 72 of POPIA.
