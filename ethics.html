Ethics & Compliance

Responsible technology. Professional accountability.

Our approach combines technology, structured systems, and human expertise to improve productivity while ensuring that accountability, ethics, and professional judgement remain firmly in the hands of qualified healthcare professionals.

Trust by design

Protecting information and strengthening confidence

Responsible healthcare systems begin with secure information practices, transparent workflows, and professionally governed processes.

  • Privacy principle

    Data Protection & Privacy

    Protecting sensitive information is fundamental to our work. MedicoTech applies strict data protection practices aligned with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and recognised data security principles.

    Our systems are designed to support the secure collection, storage, management, and handling of personal and healthcare-related information through controlled, responsible, and professionally governed processes.

  • Trust principle

    Built for Trust

    Healthcare professionals, institutions, and the individuals they serve depend on information that is accurate, consistent, and reliable.

    For this reason, ethics and professional responsibility are embedded into every aspect of our solutions and services. We design systems that support transparency, accountability, and defensible professional outputs while maintaining appropriate safeguards throughout the workflow.

Accountability, ethics, and professional judgement remain firmly in the hands of qualified healthcare professionals.

Professionals in control

Technology supports the work. Expertise directs it.

MedicoTech places qualified healthcare professionals at the centre of every workflow, decision, and outcome.

  • Technology principle

    Technology as Support, Not Replacement

    MedicoTech does not position technology as a replacement for professional expertise.

    Our digital solutions are designed to support healthcare professionals by reducing administrative burden, improving workflow efficiency, and assisting with information management and documentation processes.

    Technology helps organise, structure, and support work. Professional judgement, interpretation, decision-making, and accountability remain the responsibility of qualified healthcare professionals.

  • Oversight principle

    Human Oversight

    MedicoTech operates on a healthcare-professional-in-control principle.

    Every workflow is designed to ensure that qualified professionals remain responsible for reviewing, validating, approving, and signing off on their work. Technology supports the process, but professional expertise remains at the centre of every outcome.

    This approach helps maintain professional integrity while improving efficiency, consistency, and productivity.

Compliance & Governance

Responsible frameworks embedded into every workflow

MedicoTech is designed to operate in alignment with applicable professional, ethical, and regulatory frameworks.

Depending on the environment in which our solutions are used, this may include alignment with requirements and principles associated with:

  • POPIA

    Protection of Personal Information Act

  • HPCSA

    Health Professions Council of South Africa

  • SAHPRA

    South African Health Products Regulatory Authority

  • Ethics

    Professional ethical guidelines and codes of conduct

  • Governance

    Organisational governance and compliance requirements

Compliance is not treated as a separate activity. It is embedded into the way our systems, services, and workflows are designed and delivered.
