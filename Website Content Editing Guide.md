# Website Content Editing Guide

**MedicoTech | News articles**

Use this guide to add, update and remove articles from the News section of your website. You do not need to write code or use the Firebase console.

## Your editing workspace

Open your website address with `/admin` at the end. For example, if your website is `https://www.medicotech.co.za`, open:

**https://www.medicotech.co.za/admin**

Use the website address confirmed by your website support person. A demonstration website may have a different address.

Your workspace manages News articles only. Other pages, menus, colours and layouts are maintained by your website support person.

## Sign in

1. Enter the email address provided for your editing account.
2. Enter your password.
3. Select **Log in**.

You will see **Manage News**, with your published articles and an **Add New Article** button. Your signed-in email address and **Log out** appear near the top.

If you cannot sign in, check your email address and password. If a message says your account does not have editing permission, contact your website support person. You cannot create an account or reset a password from this screen.

## Add a new article

1. Select **Add New Article**.
2. Enter an **Article title**. Choose a short, descriptive title. The limit is 200 characters.
3. Write or paste your **Article content**.
4. Choose a **Publication date**. Use today or a past date.
5. Add images if you wish, following the instructions below.
6. Select **Save / Publish** and wait for the result message.

A successful save makes the article visible on the website immediately. The publication date controls its position in News, with newer dates first. It does not schedule a future release.

**There is no automatic saving or private draft mode.** If you are not ready to publish, prepare your text in a separate document first. Cancelling or leaving without saving discards your changes.

## Make your article easy to read

Leave a blank line between paragraphs. You can use these optional formatting shortcuts in the content box:

```text
This is the opening paragraph of the article.

## A section heading

This paragraph appears underneath the heading.

- First item
- Second item
- Third item
```

Put a space after `##` for a heading and after `-` for each list item. Leave a blank line before and after a list. Use `###` for a smaller heading if needed.

The editor does not offer a Word-style formatting toolbar. Text copied from Word will not keep all its original formatting. Website code pasted into an article is displayed as text rather than run.

## Add optional images

You may publish an article with **no images, one image or two images**. Images are never required.

- Choose a **JPEG/JPG, PNG or WebP** image.
- Each image must be **5 MB or smaller**.
- Use images you have permission to publish and avoid private or sensitive information.

To add an image:

1. In **Article images**, use the file button under **Image 1**.
2. Select an image from your computer or phone. The browser's button may say **Choose File** or similar.
3. Check the preview and filename.
4. Repeat under **Image 2** if you want a second image.
5. Select **Save / Publish**.

A preview means the image has been selected; it is not uploaded until you save. An article with one available image shows one, and an article with two available images shows both.

### If an image does not upload

The system will still try to save your article title, text and date. After saving, a message will identify any image that was not uploaded.

- If this was a new image, that image slot remains empty.
- If you were replacing an existing image, the previous image is kept.
- If the other image uploads successfully, it is still included.

When the message says **Article published successfully**, your article is live even if the same message mentions an image problem. You do not need to create the article again. Choose **Edit** later to retry the image, or leave it as a text-only article.

If you see an article-saving error instead of a successful publication message, do not assume your work was saved. Keep the editor open, copy your text somewhere safe, and contact your website support person if retrying does not help.

## Edit an existing article

1. Find the article in **Your articles**.
2. Select its **Edit** button.
3. Update the title, content or publication date.
4. Keep, replace or remove images as needed.
5. Select **Save / Publish** and wait for confirmation.

Your changes appear on the same article page after saving. Changing the publication date may move the article within the News list. The short text shown on its News card is generated from the article content. Some original articles keep their existing summary until their body text is changed.

If you change your mind, select **Cancel**. If you made changes, confirm whether you want to discard them. The previously published article stays as it was.

### Replace an image

Open **Edit**, choose a new file in the same image slot, then **Save / Publish**. You do not need to remove the old image first. If the new upload fails, the existing image is preserved.

### Remove an image

Open **Edit**, tick **Remove current image 1** or **Remove current image 2**, then **Save / Publish**. Removal only takes effect after a successful save. You can remove both images and keep the article text.

If you ticked removal by mistake, untick it before saving, or select **Cancel**. Choosing a replacement file clears the removal choice for that slot.

### If an image cannot be displayed

An existing image may temporarily be unavailable because of a connection or storage problem. The editor will show **Image preview unavailable**. This does not mean your article text has been lost.

You can keep the image unchanged, replace it or remove it. Public visitors can still read the article; unavailable images are omitted instead of showing a broken picture.

## Check the published result

Select **View website** near the top of the workspace. It opens the News page in another tab so you can keep the workspace open.

Find your article and select **Read article**. Check the title, paragraphs, date and any images. Refresh an already-open website tab to see the latest saved changes.

## Delete an article

1. Find the article in **Your articles**.
2. Select **Delete**.
3. Read the confirmation carefully.
4. Confirm only if you want to remove it permanently.

Cancelling the confirmation keeps the article. After a successful deletion, it disappears from News. There is no recycle bin or undo button in this workspace. Keep a separate copy if you may need the content again.

## When something needs attention

**Another person changed the article:** If you see a message about another session, copy any unsaved work into a separate document. Reload the workspace and reopen the latest article before applying your changes. This prevents one editor accidentally overwriting another's work.

**Connection lost while saving:** Keep the page open and wait for the result. If there is no clear success message, copy your text before reloading. Check the article list and website before creating another copy.

**Image rejected:** Check the file type and size. Try a smaller JPEG or PNG. You can still publish without it. Persistent image failures should be reported to your website support person.

**List could not refresh after saving:** The message may confirm that the change was saved but that the list could not refresh. Reload the workspace to see the latest list; do not immediately create a duplicate article.

When requesting help, include the article title, the action you tried and the exact message shown. Never send your password.

## Finish safely

Select **Log out** when you have finished, especially on a shared computer. Confirm whether to discard any unsaved changes if asked. You will return to the login page.

Your website support person manages account access and changes to other website pages. Keep their contact details with this guide.
